# Example task — historical urban footwear

**Task ID:** `EXAMPLE_ART_TASK_001`

## Objective

Create a pair of historically inspired men's urban lace-up ankle boots, approximately 1900–1910, suitable for a realistic game or cinematic character.

The goal is a coherent hero asset, not a generic modern boot with historical decoration added afterward.

## References

Use:

- `references/side.png` for profile and heel/sole relationship;
- `references/three-quarter.png` for overall volume and construction;
- `references/front.png` for toe width and lacing;
- `references/back.png` for heel counter and shaft;
- `references/pair.png` for left/right consistency.

## Visual priorities

1. Establish the correct overall silhouette and toe/shaft proportions.
2. Make the upper read as a plausible assembled leather construction.
3. Keep sole and heel thickness appropriate to the chosen reference direction.
4. Resolve lacing, eyelets, seams, and panel endings only after the primary form is stable.
5. Build restrained leather material response; avoid plastic-looking gloss.

## Required checks

Review:

- strict side view;
- front;
- back;
- top;
- three-quarter;
- close-up of lacing and seams.

Left and right footwear must remain anatomically consistent rather than being blindly mirrored if the references require asymmetry.

## Boundaries

Do not perform rigging, skinning, LOD creation, game-engine import, or unrelated scene work in this art pass.

## Endpoint

Continue the reference -> model -> viewport/render -> critique -> revision loop until the visual criteria are satisfied, then stop at **ART APPROVAL**.
